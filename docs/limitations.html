Part II: the Telugu pipeline

LimitationWeightWhat would address it
Synthetic speech only. All audio is TTS output. Nothing here speaks to accents, prosody, disfluency, speaking rate or microphone channels.FundamentalEvaluate on real recorded Telugu (an exported FLEURS or IndicVoices subset can be fed through prepare_data.py as a manifest).
Small models, by memory necessity. A larger ASR/LLM pair would likely change both WER and, above all, factual accuracy. The 0/129 result is specific to Qwen2.5-1.5B.FundamentalA GPU with room for Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct (or quantization) and Whisper-large-v3.
Templated questions. Only capitals and additions, short utterances, one fact each.ModerateOpen-ended or multi-turn questions with a human judge or semantic scorer.
Clean and noisy are not matched recordings. TTS duration prediction was not seeded, so the clean-vs-noisy gap includes rendering variance. The baseline-vs-denoised comparison is matched.ModerateSeed TTS once, save the clean waveforms, add noise to those exact files.
Quality is a lexical proxy. Word-overlap F1 does not understand paraphrase or inflection. The hallucination test is a substring heuristic, so its rate is an upper bound.ModerateA semantic or human-judged correctness score. evaluate.py accepts a custom scorer.
One deterministic run per condition. Intervals reflect question sampling only, not run-to-run, speaker or recording variance.ModerateMore question sets, speakers and TTS voices.
The noise level was chosen to create a bottleneck. 0 dB white Gaussian noise is harsher and simpler than most real conditions, and 8 dB showed no effect.DisclosedRealistic noise types (babble, reverberation, codec artifacts) at realistic SNRs.
The denoiser was tried at two settings. A negative result for this algorithm at these parameters is not a statement about denoising in general (learned enhancement models were not tried).ScopeA neural speech-enhancement front end; or fine-tuning the recognizer on noisy speech.
The ASR checkpoint is unpinned. vasista22/whisper-telugu-small resolved to two different commits for config and weights during the session, so pinning one broke loading. A future upstream change could shift results.ReproducibilityPin a consistent commit once the upstream repository stabilizes, or vendor the weights locally.
Latency is hardware-specific. RTF depends on this laptop GPU, the 256-token cap and greedy decoding. It is not streaming first-audio latency.ScopeReport on target hardware; add a streaming first-token measurement.

What can and cannot be claimed

Supported

The pipeline runs end to end. WER is 0.293 clean. Noise raises WER by a real, interval-backed amount. Denoising does not measurably change it. From speech, the 1.5B LLM never gave the right fact.

Suggestive

Aggressive spectral subtraction is worse than none (25-question slice only). The LLM, not ASR, is the main lever for quality.

Not shown

That the yielding controller works. That results hold on real speech, other languages, bigger models, or other noise. That cascades are slower than end-to-end systems.
